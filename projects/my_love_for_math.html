My Love for Math

A collection of my favorite case studies in differential equations, data fitting, mechanics, and fluid dynamics.

Why This Page Exists

Math was always my favorite subject since kindergarten. In elementary school, I treated every multiplication worksheet like a race, raised my hand as high as possible whenever the teacher would ask a question, and try my absolute hardest to learn as much math as possible. When I first used my whiteboard to write out my Algebra II homework in high school, I never went back to pen and paper. It was at this point where math was more than my favorite subject - it was a form of art that I was dying to master.

Without question, my favorite topic in mathematics is differential equations. My first introduction to differential equations was quite boring; all I could make of it was that variables need to be separated, and the "+C" is dependent on an initial condition. This premonition changed drastically when I took ME 318M (Programming and Engineering Computational Methods) with Dr. Dragan Djurdjanovic. The primary focus of this class was utilizing numerical methods to determine approximate solutions to problems which pose significantly difficulty in analytical cases. In this class, I got to witness firsthand the extent to which differential equations eloquently explain the world around us. I have featured some of my favorite use cases of differential equations which we got to analyze on this page. As I am a huge math nerd, I wholeheartedly enjoyed a great majority of Dr. Djurdjanovic's class, and I hope you enjoy seeing all the work I got to do here! Also, note that my favorite case studies are not limited to those modeled by differential equations - I also really liked curve fitting and solving systems of nonlinear equations.
